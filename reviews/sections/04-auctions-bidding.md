## Cut and why

- **Appendix "First-price and second-price auctions cost different amounts", Extension A, the budget worked examples, the strategy table, and two of the four network charts**: each re-derived a main-line result with v in place of w, or re-printed $6.3246 / $8.9443 / 0.186 / 0.677.
- **The nested closed `<details id="ar-auction-advanced">` inside the closed appendix**: two clicks to reach a calculator; flattened.
- **Roles table, inputs table, $6 CPM step table and money-flow diagram in the appendix**: these restate the main-line setup and the Chapter 1 and 3 ledgers.
- **Provenance box, ICML citation, multi-campaign/arbitrage paragraph**: these fail Q1 for a broad-understanding reader.
- **Main-line billing-basis paragraph and the long tie/unit asides**: they change no conclusion in a model where every win displays.
- **Unproved claims removed or labelled**: "projected subgradient on the dual by the envelope theorem" is removed. "Adding the spend constraint gives wF−(1+μ)c" is replaced by a 1-D derivation, a sufficiency proof and a named existence fact. Pacing convergence is labelled as illustrated.
