## Cut or moved, and why
- Bar chart "Credited, caused and break-even purchases": cut. It repeats three numbers already in the worked example and its result line; the profit-interval chart carries the visual point.
- The ITT/TOT/naive estimand table: replaced by a definitions paragraph in the main line and a full derivation in the Optional block (the table stated the naive bias without deriving it).
- Ghost-ad precision calculation, exclusion check and PSA placebo design: moved to the Optional block. They are depth on top of the core claim (divide by c; never compare exposed with control).
- The "about 0.1%" variance effect of estimating ĉ: removed. It was not derived on the page, and a correct derivation needs the delta method with a covariance term, which is beyond the brief's background.
- Generic pre-launch checklist (primary metric, MDE, guardrails, sequential stopping): cut as 101-level for this reader. The ad-specific interference is kept.
- SKAN window schedule: moved to an Optional block.
- Stale references fixed: "not the network campaign from Chapter 1" became "not the running network campaign"; "Chapter 3" for the gross spread now points to `#ar-network-spread-appendix` (Chapter 4); MMP and SSP links point to Chapter 3 with matching text.
